07 / Questions

FAQ

What is Answer Engine Optimisation?

AEO reviews how public information is discovered and represented in AI answers. Work can include technical discoverability, clearer content and repeat measurements. It does not guarantee a citation.

How is this different from SEO?

The work overlaps with SEO: crawlability, clear information and credible sources remain useful. AI visibility adds sampled checks of generated answers and their source links.

Why scope this around MHRA, GMC and ICO?

Your public claims may need internal or specialist review. We agree who approves them. Content assistance does not establish regulatory compliance or remove the need for qualified assessment.

How long until we see results?

There is no evidenced typical timeframe for Alvento engagements. We agree a baseline and reporting schedule; results may improve, remain unchanged or decline.

Do we still need traditional SEO?

Yes. Technical SEO and useful, well-supported content can contribute to AI discoverability as well as conventional search. No technique guarantees inclusion.

Can you guarantee AI citations?

No. We commit to the agreed work and transparent reporting, including unchanged or negative results. Answer engines control their own source selection.

What industries does this work for?

Organisations whose public content has to survive regulatory review: medical societies, royal colleges, clinical registries, national clinical audits and regulated medtech. If your content carries no regulatory constraint, a general SEO or AEO agency is likely the better fit.

How is a diagnostic priced?

Diagnostics are quoted against scope rather than sold off a price list. Variables that shift the number: size of the organisation, number of regulated frameworks in play (MHRA, ICO, GMC, HRA, DSPT, NICE), breadth of the answer-engine baseline, and whether the output has to clear an internal governance or legal review.
