The problem

Every QA/RA manager who has drafted a PSUR knows the pattern. You block out two or three days. You open the incident register, the complaint log, the PMCF findings, and the previous report. Then you spend the first six hours just structuring the document.

By day two you are copy-pasting between systems, reformatting tables, and manually cross-referencing UDI-DIs. The actual safety assessment, the thinking work, gets squeezed into the last afternoon because the mechanics ate the schedule.

"We spend 70 percent of the time on document assembly and 30 percent on clinical judgment. It should be the other way around."

How it works

Three steps from raw data to draft report.

01

Connect your data

Upload or paste incident reports, complaint logs, PMCF findings, and FSCA records. The tool reads structured and semi-structured inputs.

02

AI drafts every section

Benefit-risk analysis, trend summaries, aggregation tables, and narrative sections, all generated against the Article 86 template.

03

Review, approve, submit

Edit in the browser, export to PDF or DOCX, and route through your QMS for PRRC sign-off before submission to the notified body.

04

Stay compliant

Built-in MHRA alignment, MDR Article 86 structure, and version control so your technical file stays audit-ready.
