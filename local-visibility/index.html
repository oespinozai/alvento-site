Questions

Can you guarantee a top three position on Google Maps?

No one honestly can. Google decides the order. We work on the things that are known to count: a complete profile, steady reviews, consistent listings and clear local details on your site.

Do I need a new website?

Usually not. Most fixes are to your Google Business Profile and a few details on the site you already have.

How long until things change?

Profile fixes can show within weeks. Positions in the Maps results usually move over two to three months, depending on how busy your area is.

Is there a contract?

The audit is a one-off. The monthly service runs month to month.
