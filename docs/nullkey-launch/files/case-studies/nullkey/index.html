The product

A separate place for personal files.

Photos, documents and notes can end up scattered across galleries, downloads and messaging apps. NullKey brings selected items into an encrypted vault you unlock when you need them.

It is an Alvento-owned product in private testing. A public download, release date and price have not been announced.

Internal test-build screens · 20 October 2026.

How it works

Import. Lock. Back up.

Keep selected items together

Import photos and files through Android’s file picker, or write a note inside the vault. Imports keep the originals by default.

Unlock on your terms

A passphrase protects the vault. A separate recovery key provides another way to unlock it, and biometric quick unlock is optional. The app also supports automatic locking.

Create an encrypted backup

Export an encrypted backup and verify it. A recovery key unlocks an existing vault; a backup restores its contents if the device or vault is lost. You need to keep both safely.

Choose what leaves the vault

The app has no internet permission. You can export a readable copy or save an encrypted backup to storage you choose. Links open separately in your browser.

Removing originals is optional. When enabled, NullKey checks the imported copy before requesting removal. Supported photos and videos use Android’s trash confirmation; eligible local documents may be permanently deleted. It does not remove copies held by cloud services or other apps.

The build

A working app, still being tested.

NullKey demonstrates Alvento’s Android product development: local encrypted storage, recovery and backup flows, system file-picker integration and a focused interface for everyday use.

The current build uses AES-256-GCM encryption and Argon2id passphrase derivation. Its functional testing includes import, locking, backup and restore workflows. An independent security audit has not been completed.

Private testing continues on real-device behaviour and usability. Keep a separate copy of important files while testing.

For a business needing its own Android application, branding, integrations, handover and support would be scoped as a separate bespoke or white-label engagement.
