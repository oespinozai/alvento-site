Read the security model

Local encryption.

AES-256-GCM protects stored contents. Argon2id derives a key from your passphrase. The current app has no internet permission.

Your choice of unlock.

Use your passphrase, or enable supported biometric, phone screen-lock or in-app pattern unlock. A pattern is a convenience, with weaker protection.

A backup you can check.

NullKey checks the exported archive, encrypted items and thumbnails against your vault. Restore into an empty vault with the backup’s passphrase or recovery key.

Before you start

Straight answers.

Can I download NullKey now?

Yes. Get NullKey on Google Play. Android 12L or later is required.

Can Alvento read my private files?

The app sends no vault contents or decryption keys to Alvento. Your phone decrypts them when you unlock. A compromised or unlocked phone remains a risk.

What if I forget my passphrase?

A saved recovery key can open an existing vault. If the phone or files are gone, you also need a backup. Losing both secrets means we cannot reset your access.

Does importing delete the originals?

No, not by default. Optional original removal checks eligible local files first. Android asks before supported photos and videos go to trash; other supported documents may be deleted permanently. Other copies can remain.

Which phones does it work on?

NullKey requires Android 12L or later. Biometric and hardware-backed unlock options depend on the device. The app has no in-app video or PDF viewer; readable exports open outside the vault’s protection.

What can I keep in notes and documents?

Anything you would rather not leave in your gallery or a general notes app: medical letters and test results, insurance and policy details, contract copies, ID scans and short private notes. Notes and photos open inside NullKey. Other files are stored encrypted, and you open them by exporting a readable copy. NullKey is not a medical record system, so keep another copy of anything you cannot replace.
