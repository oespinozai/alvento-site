Your passphrase, recovery key and backups

Optional unlock methods you turn on, such as biometrics, your phone’s screen lock or a NullKey pattern, let anyone who can pass them open the vault. Screen-lock and pattern unlock pause after an app update or after seven days without your passphrase, and restoring a backup turns optional unlock methods off, so do not rely on them in place of your passphrase.

A recovery key opens existing data; it does not replace a backup. Keep your recovery key safe and away from your phone, keep a checked encrypted backup somewhere else, and keep the secret that opens each backup. Do not make NullKey the only copy of anything you cannot replace.

Removing originals and exporting copies

Importing a file copies it into the vault and keeps the original by default. The optional setting to remove originals deletes eligible source files on this phone only after NullKey checks that the encrypted copy matches. Photos and videos go to Android’s trash after you confirm in Android’s dialog; other supported documents are deleted permanently. Deleting is not a guaranteed secure erase, and cloud, synced or other copies may remain.

A readable copy you export, and text you copy to the clipboard, are outside NullKey’s protection and may be kept by other apps. Check what you select before you confirm. You are responsible for what you choose to delete or export.
