The honest case for Hide Photos, Video-Hide it Pro

Some readers may find Hide Photos, Video-Hide it Pro the closer fit. Its Google Play listing lists 'Free Cloud backup of your files' as a feature, and NullKey has no sync, so readers who want a cloud backup feature will find one listed there and not in NullKey. The listing also says the app hides music, apps, calls, messages and 'any other files', while NullKey's vault holds notes, documents, photos and other files you import.

Readers who want a disguised app will find that in the listing too. It says the app is disguised as 'Audio Manager' in the app drawer, that other disguises are available, that the app icon can be hidden, and that an escape PIN or password is available. NullKey has no disguise or decoy option.

Unlock options on each app

NullKey offers an optional strong-biometric unlock, and phone screen-lock unlock and an in-app pattern are also available on supported devices. Hide Photos, Video-Hide it Pro's listing mentions fingerprint unlock and PIN, password and pattern lock options.

Ads and purchase labels

Hide Photos, Video-Hide it Pro's Google Play listing carries the label 'Contains ads' and the label 'In-app purchases'. NullKey has no ads. The listing also says 'COMPLETELY FREE and UNLIMITED'.

NullKey is free for up to 30 items in total. Its one optional one-time unlock removes that limit, and there is no subscription.

Where each app is available

NullKey requires Android 12L or later and comes from Google Play. Hide Photos, Video-Hide it Pro is installed from Google Play, and its listing also links to a browser extension for PC and Mac and to a separate app for other devices.

Bottom line

Choose Hide Photos, Video-Hide it Pro if a cloud backup feature, a disguised app icon or the listed kinds of hidden items, including music, apps and messages, are what you need, as its Google Play listing describes them. Choose NullKey if you want a vault for private notes, documents and photos with no sync, no disguise and no ads.

Frequently asked questions

Does Hide Photos, Video-Hide it Pro offer cloud backup?

Its Google Play listing lists 'Free Cloud backup of your files' as a feature. NullKey has no sync, and you can save an encrypted backup wherever you choose with Android's file picker.

Can NullKey disguise itself or show a decoy?

No. NullKey has no disguise or decoy option. Hide Photos, Video-Hide it Pro's listing says the app is disguised as 'Audio Manager' in the app drawer, lists other disguises, and offers an option to hide the app icon and an escape PIN or password.

Does each app offer in-app purchases?

Hide Photos, Video-Hide it Pro's Google Play listing carries the label 'In-app purchases' and also says 'COMPLETELY FREE and UNLIMITED'. NullKey has one optional one-time unlock bought through Google Play, with no subscription, and it is free for up to 30 items in total.
