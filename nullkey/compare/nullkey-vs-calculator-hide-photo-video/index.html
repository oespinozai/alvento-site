NullKey vs Calculator Hide Photo, Video

NullKey is an Android app from Google Play, needing Android 12L or later, that keeps private photos, files and notes in an encrypted vault on the phone, with no ads. Calculator Hide Photo, Video is an Android app from Google Play that stores files, notes and contacts as well as photos and videos behind a calculator front. The main differences are the calculator disguise, the backup route and the kinds of content each one holds.

The honest case for Calculator Hide Photo, Video

Calculator Hide Photo, Video is a better fit for readers who want the app disguised as a regular calculator. Its Google Play listing says the vault opens only after a numeric PIN is entered on the calculator. NullKey does not disguise itself and does not show a decoy.

It also fits readers who want contacts kept alongside their photos and videos. Its listing says images, videos, audio and files can be imported, and it describes an export icon in the vault that restores hidden media. NullKey's export options are a whole-vault encrypted backup and a readable export of single items.

Backup, sync and changing phones

NullKey has no sync, and the current app declares no internet permission. You can save an encrypted backup wherever you choose with Android's file picker, and NullKey runs a read-back check on it.

Calculator Hide Photo, Video's listing says its files are stored only on the device, and asks users to back up hidden files before moving to a new device or doing a factory reset. Its listing also describes an export icon in the vault that restores hidden media.

Disguise and how the vault opens

Calculator Hide Photo, Video is disguised as a regular calculator. Its listing says the vault is opened by entering a numeric PIN on the calculator.

NullKey does not disguise itself or show a decoy. It opens with your passphrase, plus optional biometric, screen-lock or in-app pattern unlock where the phone supports them.

What each vault holds and shows

NullKey lists private notes, documents and photos. Photos and notes open inside the app. Other files are stored encrypted, and you open them by exporting a readable copy, which sits outside the vault's protection.

Calculator Hide Photo, Video's listing says it stores files, notes and contacts as well as photos and videos. The listing also shows the label 'Contains ads', and NullKey has no ads.

Bottom line

Choose Calculator Hide Photo, Video if a calculator disguise and a numeric PIN entered on the calculator matter most to you, or if you want files, notes and contacts in one app. Choose NullKey if you want a whole-vault encrypted backup that the app checks after saving, and an app with no ads and no disguise.
