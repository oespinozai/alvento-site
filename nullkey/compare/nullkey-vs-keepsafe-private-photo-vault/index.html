NullKey vs Private Photo Vault - Keepsafe

NullKey is an Android vault for private notes, documents and photos with no account, no ads and no sync. Private Photo Vault - Keepsafe is an Android app installed from Google Play for photos, videos and documents, with optional backup to a private Keepsafe cloud through a Keepsafe account.

The honest case for Keepsafe

Keepsafe is the better fit for readers who want items backed up to a cloud and synced across devices. Its help page says that with a Keepsafe account you can back up items to your own private Keepsafe cloud, and that items sync across devices when backup is on.

Its help page describes it for iPhone, Android and tablets, while NullKey runs on Android 12L or later. It also suits readers who want an email-based reset or a disguise option. Its help page says a forgotten PIN is reset with an access code sent to the account email address. Its Premium features include Secret Door, which disguises the app as a different kind of app on Android only, and Decoy Vault, a second gallery with a separate PIN.

Where backups and sync go

NullKey has no sync, and the current app declares no internet permission. You can save an encrypted backup wherever you choose with Android's file picker, and whole-vault backups include a read-back check.

Keepsafe's Help Center describes optional encrypted backup to its own private cloud, with sync across devices when backup is on. Its help page also says selected items can be exported to the device gallery on Android, and that exported files are decrypted.

How each app lets you back in

NullKey has no account, and it uses a recovery key that you save yourself. There is no account reset.

The Help Center describes creating a Keepsafe login, with a name and an email address that is verified, as the first step for new users. Its help page says a forgotten PIN is reset with an access code sent to the account email address.

Unlock, ads and paid features

NullKey offers optional strong-biometric unlock, and phone screen-lock unlock and an in-app pattern are also available on supported devices. It has no ads, one optional one-time unlock with no subscription, and a free tier for up to 30 items in total.

Keepsafe's Google Play listing says the app unlocks with a PIN, pattern or fingerprint on Android, and carries the labels contains ads and in-app purchases. Its free version is ad supported, and ad-free is a Premium feature.

Bottom line

Choose Private Photo Vault - Keepsafe if you want optional cloud backup and sync across devices, an email-based PIN reset, or the Premium disguise and decoy options. Choose NullKey if you want a vault with no account, no ads and no sync, and a recovery key that you save yourself.

Frequently asked questions

Does Keepsafe need an account to back up?

Its help page says that with a Keepsafe account you have the option to back up items to your own private Keepsafe cloud. NullKey has no account and no sync, and you can save an encrypted whole-vault backup wherever you choose with Android's file picker.

How is the stored data encrypted in each app?

Keepsafe's help page says its files are protected with 256-bit AES encryption and are encrypted at rest. NullKey's contents use AES-256-GCM, with a separate random key for each item, and the vault's keys are protected by a key that Argon2id derives from your passphrase.

What happens if I forget my unlock secret?

Keepsafe's help page says a forgotten PIN is reset with an access code sent to the account email address. NullKey has no account reset, and it uses a recovery key that you save yourself.
