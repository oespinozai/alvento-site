A hidden folder and an encrypted vault do different jobs

A hidden folder only changes what is visible, while an encrypted vault protects stored files with a secret. Check what is encrypted and how backups work.

Hiding is a visibility choice

An album can disappear from the usual gallery view while its files remain available elsewhere. Do not infer encryption from a hidden icon or a folder name. Check the app’s own documentation for what is encrypted, how it unlocks and what happens to backups.

Ask four questions

What protects the stored files? Who holds the keys? What happens if I forget the secret? How will I move to another phone? Answers are more useful than labels such as “military-grade”. A vault should explain its limits as plainly as its features.

Frequently asked questions

What should I ask before trusting a private folder?

Ask what encrypts the stored items, who controls the keys, what you can do if you forget the secret, and how you would migrate to another phone. Vague labels are not an answer, so prefer a product that explains its limits as plainly as its features.

Does a hidden icon mean the files inside are encrypted?

No. Hiding can make an album vanish from the regular gallery, yet its contents may still be reachable through other routes, so a hidden icon or folder name says nothing about encryption. Look for a written account of what is encrypted, how the folder unlocks, and what happens to its backups.
