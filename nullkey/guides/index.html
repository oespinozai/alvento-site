Backups and recovery

Plan how to keep copies and recover access.

How do you back up a vault that stays offline?

Offline does not mean backed up. Create an encrypted backup in Settings, wait for the read-back check, and keep it away from the phone with its secret.

Recovery key or backup? You need both jobs covered

A recovery key unlocks the vault but is not a copy of your data. A backup holds the data and still needs a valid secret to open it.

How often should you back up an encrypted vault?

There is no fixed schedule. Back up after changes you would miss, and keep each backup's own passphrase or recovery key apart from the backup file.

How do you test a backup restore before you need it?

Restore a backup of sample files into an empty vault on a spare phone, then check the contents and the secret before you rely on the backup.

What can you do if you forget a photo vault password?

What happens if you lose your recovery key and passphrase?

If you lose both secrets, nobody can reset them. An enabled unlock method may still open the vault; otherwise only a backup with its own secret helps.
