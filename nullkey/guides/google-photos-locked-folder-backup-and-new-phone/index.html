Google Photos Locked Folder: backup, new phone and limits

How Google Photos Locked Folder is protected, how its backup works, and what Google says is deleted on a reset, clearing app data or removing the app.

Check which Locked Folder items are backed up

A private photo is only safe from loss if a copy exists somewhere other than the phone it was taken on. Before you rely on any private folder, find out who can open it and which of its items are backed up. It takes a few checks, and it is easier to do before a reset or a phone change than after.

This guide sets out what Google Photos Help says about Locked Folder on Android, then how NullKey handles the same questions.

What protects Locked Folder

Google says you can save sensitive photos and videos to a folder protected by your device screen lock in the Google Photos app, and that your Locked Folder password is the same as your device screen lock. Google also says Locked Folder is protected by Google Photos' standard encryption practices, and that its items are not available to other apps on your device that have access to your photos and videos.

Two consequences follow. Anyone who knows your phone's screen lock can open Locked Folder. And Google says that to share a photo or video stored in Locked Folder, you must first move it out of the folder.

How Locked Folder backup works

Google says you can turn on automatic backup for your Locked Folder from Photos settings, then Backup, then Back up Locked Folder, and that you must first turn on backup for the rest of your photos and videos. Google also says you can keep Locked Folder backup off and still back up individual items in Locked Folder by hand.

Google says that items in Locked Folder that you don't back up will only be stored on that device. If backup is off, Google says Locked Folder items are deleted if you factory reset your device, clear app data or delete the Photos app.

So the question to answer is item by item: for each photo you care about, is it backed up, either automatically or by hand? If not, it exists only on that phone.

Check your Locked Folder

Run through these on your own phone before any reset, app clean-up or phone change.

  • Open Photos settings, then Backup, then Back up Locked Folder, and check whether it is on.
  • If it is off, check whether the individual items you care about were backed up by hand.
  • Keep another copy of anything irreplaceable that is not backed up.
  • Remember that anyone with your screen lock can open Locked Folder.

Frequently asked questions

Is Google Photos Locked Folder backed up?

Only if you back it up. Google says you can turn on automatic Locked Folder backup once main backup is on, or back up individual items by hand. Items you don't back up are stored only on that device.

Why did my Locked Folder photos disappear?

Google says that if backup is off, Locked Folder items are deleted if you factory reset your device, clear app data or delete the Photos app.

Can someone else open my Locked Folder?

Google says the Locked Folder password is the same as your device screen lock, so anyone who knows your screen lock can open it.
