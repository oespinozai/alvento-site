How do you back up a vault that stays offline?

An offline app still needs a recovery plan. Here is how to build one.

Offline does not mean backed up

A vault stored only on a phone can disappear with the phone. Encryption protects confidentiality; it does not protect against loss, uninstalling an app or failing storage. Plan for losing the entire device.

Export ciphertext, not a folder of originals

An encrypted vault backup preserves protected files in an export you can store elsewhere. A decrypted export creates another readable copy. Know which operation you are using. In NullKey, the whole-vault backup contains ciphertext and requires the passphrase or recovery key from that vault.

Check the backup, then practise restoring

NullKey rereads the exported archive, authenticates its index, every encrypted item segment and every thumbnail, and matches the current vault snapshot. A restore exercise with sample data also checks that you have the right secret and understand the process. Restore requires an empty vault; never erase a working vault just to try it.

Make the routine repeatable

Keep an encrypted backup away from the phone and the unlocking secret separately. Refresh the backup after important changes. Label which passphrase or recovery key belongs to older exports: changing the current vault’s credentials does not rewrite backup files you already made.
