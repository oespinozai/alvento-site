NullKey by Alvento. Built around private files that stay with you.

Made by Alvento

NullKey is an Android product by Alvento Ltd, a UK company, number 16394298. We build the app around on-device encryption and secrets you hold.

Contact and support

Email hello@alvento.uk with “NullKey” in the subject. For a problem with the app, tell us the app version, Android version and the steps that led to it.

Do not send passphrases, recovery keys, backups or private vault contents. Use sample data if you need to describe a problem. For a security issue, use the subject “NullKey security”.

App and website privacy

The offline app and this website are separate systems. Read NullKey’s published app privacy policy for the vault, unlock methods, imports, backups and support correspondence.

This product website has no signup form, file upload, tracking analytics or advertising scripts. This product section is hosted with Alvento’s main website. Hosting services may process request and operational information to serve pages. Visiting a website still makes network requests, even though the app itself works offline.

Alvento’s website privacy notice describes the company website and contact services.
