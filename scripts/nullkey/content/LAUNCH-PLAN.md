# Organic launch plan — working draft

## Direction from Oscar

Make it like the transcript app, but better. Vaultaire is the product and distribution reference: a simple private photo/file vault, immediate demonstration, a clear download path, useful question-led guides, feature pages, fair comparisons and internal links. Do not reposition it as a niche document-storage product.

“Better” means specific, testable improvements: understand the product quickly, distinguish encryption from hiding, explain backup and recovery clearly, reduce download friction and measure the funnel. It does not mean claiming stronger security without evidence or adding feature breadth.

## What exists
Private marketing preview: homepage, security overview, brand study, guide hub and six original draft guides. Static HTML with responsive styles. No analytics, signup form, invented reviews or download placeholders. Site explicitly remains in development and noindex.

## Before public launch
1. Confirm NullKey naming, domain availability and appropriate brand clearance.
2. Confirm release features, support contact, legal operator, website privacy notice and app privacy policy.
3. Decide distribution and pricing. Add real Play listing or an authorised test download; do not imply availability before it exists.
4. Replace the illustrative phone with actual screenshots. Produce a concise demo using sample files.
5. Review the six guides against the release. Verify external links and Android behaviour on supported devices.
6. Select a production domain, add canonical URLs, sitemap, appropriate metadata/social assets and remove noindex only for the public site. Private preview URLs should never be used as SEO canonical URLs.
7. Configure Search Console and a privacy-conscious measurement plan. Website download-link clicks, store engagement, installs and purchases are different metrics. Document attribution limits.

## Seed content, then feedback
The six guides are an initial editorial cluster, not validated keyword targets. Research Android query demand and current search results before expanding. Do not publish near-duplicate pages for every phrasing. Search Console becomes useful after the site gains impressions; initial topic discovery needs broader keyword and audience research.

Initial pages:
- Where should private documents live on Android?
- A hidden folder and an encrypted vault do different jobs.
- How do you back up a vault that stays offline?
- Before deleting the original, check the copy.
- Recovery key or backup? You need both jobs covered.
- What does “no account” mean for a private vault?

## First exposure experiments
1. Product demonstration: one short recording, clear captions and real export/recovery workflow.
2. Community feedback: prepare a transparent maker introduction and practical explanation for relevant Android/privacy spaces. Check each community's rules before posting. No automated unsolicited promotion.
3. Reviewer outreach: identify small Android/privacy creators whose audience fits; personalise a sample invitation after distribution is ready. No messages have been sent.
4. Comparison content: document built-in options fairly using primary sources and actual tests. No blanket claims that competing products are insecure.
5. Directories: select only relevant listings once real distribution and policies exist. Paid newswire exposure is not a substitute for evidence of use.

## Weekly review
Track index coverage, non-brand impressions/clicks, query relevance, engaged guide visits where measurable, product-page visits, outbound distribution clicks and available store/purchase reports. Use cohorts over time; do not import the interview's claimed 10% website conversion or 5.47% day-one paid conversion as targets.

Decision logic: no impressions -> investigate discovery/indexing and topics; impressions without clicks -> check intent/title/snippet; guide visits without product interest -> inspect relevance and CTA; product interest without store action -> inspect clarity and distribution friction. Small sample sizes are directional only.

## Reference-led next content wave
Research these Android questions before writing; these are hypotheses, not proven keywords:
- How do I keep private photos on Android?
- Does hiding a photo encrypt it?
- Are private photos still in my cloud backup?
- How do I move photos into an encrypted vault?
- Can I use a photo vault without an account?
- What happens to my vault when I change phones?

Add dedicated pages for implemented features: photo/file encryption, optional biometric unlock, recovery key, encrypted backup and private notes. Add comparisons only after testing relevant Android alternatives. Link guides to the relevant feature and the eventual real download destination.

## Better-than-reference acceptance criteria
- Hero immediately identifies an Android photo/file vault and shows the experience.
- Primary CTA becomes a real install/test action when distribution is ready; until then it opens the demonstration, not a fake download.
- Product demonstration is visible near the top; final screenshots are genuine.
- Recovery instructions distinguish unlocking existing data from restoring lost data.
- No fabricated reviews, rankings, traffic or unsupported comparative claims.
- Expand localisation after English content and conversion show evidence of working.
- Maintain a query research -> useful page -> contextual internal links -> download -> cohort review loop. One article a day is a capacity choice, not a ranking guarantee.
