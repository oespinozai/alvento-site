Staamp / Customer loyalty

Your regulars deserve better than a paper card.

A coffee before work. A lunchtime sandwich. The next haircut. Repeat visits are the heartbeat of an independent business.

We built Staamp to put loyalty cards on customers’ phones and give staff a simple way to approve visits and track rewards. It’s live at Lizette’s, an independent coffee shop—and provides a foundation we can adapt for salons, barbers, delis and other local businesses.

At Lizette’s

The coffee is personal. The loyalty card should be too.

At an independent coffee shop, regulars matter. A paper card keeps count, but gives the shop little to work with beyond the next stamp. For Lizette’s, we brought the card onto customers’ phones and put staff in control of every approval.
