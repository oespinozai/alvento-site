01, The problem

A paper stamp card can't tell a shop who its regulars are.

Paper cards get lost, forgotten, or forged, and a small shop never learns who's actually coming back, how often, or when they've drifted off. In a village, the customer base is small and mostly known by name already. The job here isn't chasing new volume. It's proving the regulars keep coming back, and building a channel to reach them when they don't.

Generic loyalty apps are built for chains with thousands of locations and marketing budgets to match. Almost none of them are built for a single-site shop where thirty customers is the whole business.
