Staamp: an unpaid live pilot with an independent deli

Staamp is a live pilot with an independent deli. The pilot is unpaid.

What Staamp does

Staamp is a digital loyalty-card system. Participants open their card in a phone browser, staff approve stamps, and the deli manages the scheme through a staff dashboard.
