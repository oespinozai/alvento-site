Clinical / registry · React · Supabase · Postgres RLS

Reads like a clinical instrument, not a SaaS dashboard: an append-only ledger, row-level isolation, retention rules enforced in the schema rather than hoped for in policy. Built and owned by Alvento to show what a regulated-medtech record system should look like.
