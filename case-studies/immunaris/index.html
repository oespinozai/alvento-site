Follow the record

Four steps. One connected workflow.

See how a patient moves from registration to a vaccination record and a certificate. Each screen focuses on the task at hand. The reference build uses sample data.

01 / Find the patient

Find the patient. See what’s due.

A register brings patients and their vaccination status into view. Explore the cohort and due-date information your team would need to plan the next step.

02 / Gather the details

Get the right details from the start.

The intake screen brings identity, allergies, vaccination history and consent details into one structured form, so the team has a clear starting point.

03 / Record the dose

Record the dose while the details are fresh.

Product, batch, administration site, clinician and time belong together. The recording screen shows how to capture those details as part of the same workflow.

04 / Complete the handover

Turn the record into a useful handover.

Explore the certificate view and how a recorded vaccination can become a document to share. We can adapt the output to the information your service needs to hand over.

Make it yours

Your service has its own workflow. Let’s build for it.

Immunaris shows one vaccination workflow. The same approach to structured intake, clear records and useful handovers can inform a system for your service. We start with the tasks your team performs and the decisions the record needs to support.

Designed for the task

Less hunting for a field. More clarity at each step.

The register, intake form and certificate use a consistent layout: readable type, clearly grouped fields and a focused view of the task. The aim is to make the next action easy to find and the resulting record easy to read.

For a live service, we work through the clinical and information-governance requirements with your team and test the agreed controls. This reference demo is a way to explore the workflow and design.

Behind the build

A foundation your team can build on.

The interface uses React and TypeScript, with structured forms and reusable components. The implementation stack includes Supabase and Postgres; live authentication, permissions, record history and integrations are part of the deployment scope.
