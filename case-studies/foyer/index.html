Reference build: phone reception

Foyer, AI phone reception for busy small businesses.

When you’re with a customer or out on a job, the phone still rings. Foyer demonstrates how AI can handle routine booking conversations, collect caller details and pass requests to your team when a person is needed.

01, The problem

Keep enquiries moving while you get on with the work.

For a sign maker, repair business, tradesperson or small practice, answering the phone can mean interrupting the customer or job in front of you. After closing, enquiries may wait until someone is available to respond.

A useful first response collects the details your team needs and makes the next step clear. This demo uses appointment booking to show the approach; quotation enquiries and service requests need their own business-specific questions and follow-up process.

We agree what Foyer can handle, what needs a person and what happens when nobody is available before connecting a live phone line.

04, The approach

Seven decisions that keep it trustworthy on a live line.

A voice agent fails the moment it either sounds like a robot reading a script, or pretends competence it doesn't have. These decisions are what keep it useful instead of embarrassing.

Decision 01

Answer first, qualify second

In a live deployment, calls can be routed to Foyer during busy periods or outside opening hours. The first exchange establishes what the caller needs. Capacity and fallback routing are agreed during setup.

Decision 02

Booking is a live function call, not a promise

The demo uses simulated availability. In a connected deployment, a booking should only be confirmed after the calendar accepts it. If that fails, the caller gets a clear next step rather than an unconfirmed appointment.

Decision 03

Knows when to hand off

Requests outside the agreed scope are passed to your designated team. We define transfer and callback rules, including what happens if nobody answers. The demo does not provide clinical advice or an emergency service.

Decision 04

Every call logged, nothing lost

Transcript and outcome are recorded automatically for every call, so a missed detail is a two-minute review, not a mystery.

Decision 05

A changed mind doesn't break the flow

"Actually, can we do Tuesday instead?" is handled as a normal turn in the conversation, not a script restart. The call keeps its shape even when the caller doesn't.

Decision 06

Every call becomes CRM data, automatically

Calendar and CRM connections are checked individually during scoping. We confirm what your software supports before promising automatic booking or data sync; supported workflows are tested before activation.

Decision 07

A confirmation, not just a call

In a live deployment, appointment confirmations can go by email or SMS once the booking succeeds. The practice gets a separate call summary and access to the transcript, so the team knows what happened and what needs attention.

Getting started

Built around your business.

Can I keep my phone number?

We check your provider’s call-forwarding options so you can keep your existing number where supported. You choose whether Foyer covers busy periods, out-of-hours calls or a wider schedule.

Will it work with my software?

Tell us which calendar, CRM or job-management system you use. We verify the connection and booking permissions before agreeing the scope. The public demo is not connected to a real business.

What happens when a person is needed?

We agree who receives the request, how they are notified and what callers are told if nobody is available. Human answering staff are not included in this reference build.

What does it cost?

We quote for setup and ongoing service after checking your call volume, required workflows and integrations. You receive the scope, usage allowance and any additional charges before committing.
