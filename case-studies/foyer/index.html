Foyer / AI phone reception

Be with your customer. Be there for your next one.

The phone rings while you’re halfway through a job. Foyer shows how an AI receptionist can take the conversation from there: collect the details, work through a booking request and recognise when your team needs to step in. Hear it handle a change of plan. Then give it one yourself.

Hear the demo Try it yourself Talk about your phone line

The demo uses fictional businesses and a simulated calendar. No real appointments are made. We scope live phone, calendar and follow-up connections around your business.

Getting started

A few practical questions.

Can I keep my phone number?

We check your provider's call-forwarding options so you can keep your existing number where supported. You choose whether Foyer covers busy periods, out-of-hours calls or a wider schedule.

Will it work with my software?

Tell us which calendar, CRM or job-management system you use. We verify the connection and booking permissions before agreeing the scope.

What happens when a person is needed?

We agree who receives the request, how they are notified and what callers are told if nobody is available. Human answering staff are not included in this reference build.

What does it cost?

We quote for setup and ongoing service after checking your call volume, required workflows and integrations. You receive the scope, usage allowance and any additional charges before committing.
