The details that matter

Follow up with confidence. Keep the awkward surprises out.

Question 01

Will it send an embarrassing duplicate?

Each invoice gets one reminder per stage. Run the cycle twice on the same demo date: the second run creates no duplicates. A repeated payment event is also recognised and ignored.

Question 02

Will it chase someone who has paid?

Once a payment matches and the invoice is marked paid, later reminder runs skip it. Try the simulated payment, then run the cycle again to see the result.

Question 03

Can I see exactly what went out?

Open any captured message in the demo outbox. The ledger, messages and activity trail stay together, so you can inspect the wording and see what happened.

Question 04

What happens when a payment needs checking?

The workflow checks the invoice reference, customer, amount and currency. A payment that cannot be matched to one invoice is held for a person to review.

The right wording

Polite by design. Firmer by schedule.

The first reminder can be a gentle nudge. A later one needs a clear request for a payment date. Arrears demonstrates six stages, from day 7 to day 42, with the wording agreed in advance. You can inspect each message in the demo.

We can adapt the timing and tone to your terms and customer relationships, with a route to your team for disputes and exceptions.

Getting started

Fit the follow-up to the way you work.

Can this work with our invoicing process?

Tell us where invoices and payment records live. We check the available integrations and agree how the ledger will stay up to date before scoping a live implementation.

Can we change the reminder schedule?

Yes. The demo shows days 7, 14, 21, 28, 35 and 42. We can adapt the timing, wording and escalation rules to your terms and approval process.

Does the demo contact anyone or take payments?

No. It uses fictional invoices, captured emails and simulated payments. A live rollout needs an agreed invoice source, payment connection and email delivery setup.

Behind the build

A clear record of every step.

Python and Flask run the reminder workflow. SQLite keeps the invoice ledger, captured messages and payment events together. The Stripe SDK verifies signatures on payment events; this demo uses signed test fixtures. A daily timer runs the same reminder logic as the controls above.
